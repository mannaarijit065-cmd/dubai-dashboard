How to read off-plan vs secondary PSF: Off-plan launch PSF = average price at developer launch (pre-construction risk, payment plan spread). Secondary PSF = average ready/resale transaction PSF for that community. New off-plan launches are typically 5–20% below secondary ready prices in established communities. The "CAGR" column shows compounded annual growth from launch year to today.  |  Data variance note (important): Secondary PSF figures shown here are sourced from Bayut/PropertyFinder listing averages and DLD-reported ready-only transactions. DLD's consolidated area-level PSF (all transaction types including off-plan, via DXBInteract/DXBAnalytics) typically runs 8–15% higher because it blends premium off-plan launches into the average. Verified examples from DXBAnalytics Jan 2026 DLD data: Town Square (Al Yelayiss 2) DLD all-txn avg = AED 1,401–1,808/sqft vs our ready-only ~AED 1,130–1,250; JVC DLD = AED 1,473/sqft vs our ~AED 1,260; Business Bay DLD = AED 2,901/sqft vs our ~AED 1,960 (DLD figure heavily skewed by Binghatti Skyrise off-plan launches at AED 2,577–2,660); Dubai South DLD = AED 1,415/sqft vs our ~AED 920 (ready-only). For pure secondary/ready PSF, our figures are the more relevant benchmark for resale decisions. For overall market heat, use the DXBInteract/DLD all-transaction figure. Cross-check both at dxbanalytics.com.

Data honesty: Off-plan launch PSF is the average PSF across all developer transactions in that community/year — not the highest or lowest. Secondary PSF is the average of all ready/resale transactions. Both sourced from DLD open data (via DXBInteract), Bayut's 2025 Annual Sales Report, Knight Frank Q1 2025 Review, and Betterhomes 2025 Market Report.  |  The "launch vs secondary" column shows whether the average off-plan launch price is above (+) or below (−) average secondary price. Negative = developer selling at a discount to the secondary market (healthy off-plan investment thesis). Positive = developer pricing above secondary (common in luxury/branded segments or where off-plan stock is newer quality than aging secondary).  |  Geopolitical adjustment (April 2026): Israel-Iran tensions, US tariff uncertainty → luxury/prime 5–10% headwind; mid-market more resilient; outer community appreciation 2–4% drag vs baseline. Projections reflect this.  |  Verified = sourced from named reports. Estimated = interpolated. Projected = forecast with stated assumptions.
